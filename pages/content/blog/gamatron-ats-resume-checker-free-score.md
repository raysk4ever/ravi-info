## What Does the Free ATS Scan Actually Check?

The checker divides the score into two halves. The 60-point structure and format section looks for contact details, standard section headings, sensible length, real bullet points, quantified achievements, and layout traps such as tables. The 40-point keyword section compares your resume with the job description and names the critical keywords you are missing.

That split is useful because the two halves fail for different reasons. A resume can be clean and well written but still miss the vocabulary the employer searches for. The reverse is also common: a keyword-stuffed resume in a two-column template can be parsed badly before a human ever reads it. ## What Does a Fix Look Like in Practice?

The most common structural problem is a bullet that describes a duty instead of an outcome. A scan flags the missing number; the rewrite supplies it. Here is a realistic before-and-after pair — the figures are illustrative, not output from a real resume:

![Before and after resume bullet rewrite suggested by an ATS scan](/blog-images/gamatron-ats-resume-checker-free-score-2.jpg)
*A generic bullet becomes a specific one when the scan asks for a measurable result.*

```text
Before: Responsible for improving the onboarding flow.
After:  Reduced onboarding drop-off from 38% to 24% by rebuilding
        the signup steps and adding progress persistence.
```

The second version keeps the same job but adds a metric and a method, which gives both an ATS and a recruiter something concrete to match. A keyword gap looks different: if the posting says " incident response" and your resume says "on-call support," the checker will name the missing term and you decide whether it is honest to add it. Do not add skills you cannot discuss in an interview.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an ATS?

An applicant tracking system is software employers use to collect, parse, and search resumes. It reads your resume into structured fields and ranks candidates partly by keyword match. Understanding that parsing step explains why standard headings and simple layouts matter more than visual flair.

### Is the ATS checker really free?

Yes. The quick scan is free and needs no account. It returns the score, missing keywords, and formatting issues. The deep analysis with line-by-line bullet rewrites and the one-click handoff to the Resume Builder need a free Gamatron account.

### Is my resume saved?

The scan runs on the server and the result is returned to you. Gamatron does not save the resume for anonymous scans. The text is sent to an AI model provider to compute the keyword match, so avoid pasting anything confidential into any online checker.

### Which file types work?

PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown, and HTML are accepted. A scanned PDF with no selectable text cannot be read, so export a text-based PDF instead. If in doubt, paste the plain text of the resume into the form and skip the upload step entirely.
